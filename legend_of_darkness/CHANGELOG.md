# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- 26 forest encounters (up from 8), many with choices and several possible outcomes:
  treasure chests and mimics, glowing mushrooms, a crossroads witch, goblin dice,
  bandit tolls, an old well, a cursed tome, a wyrm hatchling's hoard, your own shadow,
  the Lady of the Silver Pool, a merchant caravan and more.
- Some encounters only appear at higher levels; some lead to bonus fights with extra rewards.
- Every encounter now shows a summary of what you gained or lost.
- Encounters are slightly more common (15% of forest searches, up from 12%).

## 0.1.0

- First release: village, forest, combat, shops, healer, inn, bank, training masters,
  the Shadow Wyrm, player ambushes, the Shades, daily news, hall of fame and village chat.
