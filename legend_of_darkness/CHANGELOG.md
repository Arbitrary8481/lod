# Changelog

## 0.4.1

- The forge and armoury now list upgrades and downgrades separately, marked with
  ▲ / ▼ and the stat change. Prices shown are what you pay after trade-in, items you
  can't afford are greyed out, and the best upgrade you can afford is called out.
- Downgrading asks you to confirm, and every purchase shows your stat before and after.

## 0.4.0

- The town square now shows the date on the village notice board.
- Behind-the-scenes tools for the game's owner.

## 0.3.1

- High/Low now deals each hand from a real shuffled 52-card deck instead of rolling every
  card fresh, so matches are rarer (about 6% of guesses instead of 7.7%) and no card
  repeats within a hand. Payouts are worked out from the cards actually left in the deck.

## 0.3.0

- High/Low card game with One-Eyed Hesk at The Gloaming Lantern. Bet, then guess whether
  the next card is higher or lower; each right guess multiplies your pot by the odds.
  Cash out any time. Bets are limited to 100 gold per level, pots to 20x the stake, and
  any unfinished pot is paid back at the new day. Big winning streaks make the Daily News.

## 0.2.1

- Encounters no longer waste a reward on healing when you're already at full health:
  healing-only outcomes are skipped, the healing spring doesn't appear, and the caravan
  doesn't offer its healing draught.

## 0.2.0

- 26 forest encounters (up from 8), many with choices and several possible outcomes:
  treasure chests and mimics, glowing mushrooms, a crossroads witch, goblin dice,
  bandit tolls, an old well, a cursed tome, a wyrm hatchling's hoard, your own shadow,
  the Lady of the Silver Pool, a merchant caravan and more.
- Some encounters only appear at higher levels; some lead to bonus fights with extra rewards.
- Every encounter now shows a summary of what you gained or lost.
- Encounters are slightly more common (15% of forest searches, up from 12%).

## 0.1.0

- First release: village, forest, combat, shops, healer, inn, bank, training masters,
  the Shadow Wyrm, player ambushes, the Shades, daily news, hall of fame and village chat.
